lib/authn/distribution.js ports relaycheck's identity probe set v2 (prompts, labels, aliases), its answer
normalization (src/probes/classify.ts), its statistics (src/stats.ts, src/rng.ts) and its billing baseline prompts
(src/probes/billing.ts); lib/authn/distribution-refs.json carries its reference fingerprints recorded on OpenAI's API
(refs/, commit 43d9294e2a121dbbbc0a145220c016dda9d26ae4). Source: https://github.com/YanYunCY/relaycheck
